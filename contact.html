Consultation Expectations

What occurs during the initial discovery?

The initial 45-minute consultation is a relaxed, exploratory dialogue focused on understanding your objectives, assessing portfolio structure, and determining if there is a mutual fiduciary fit.

What documents should I prepare?

No documents are mandatory for the first discussion. If you wish to receive a high-level second opinion, having current asset allocation summaries on hand can be beneficial.

Is there any financial commitment?

None. The discovery consultation is entirely without obligation or cost. We believe trust must be earned through intellectual rigor before any advisory agreement is considered.
